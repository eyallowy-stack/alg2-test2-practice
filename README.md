# Algebra 2 Test 2 practice

Adaptive practice for a 10th-grade Honors Algebra 2 test: inverse functions, describing functions in words (including inverses and composites), slope, average rate of change, equations of lines, line word problems, line of best fit and correlation, and piecewise linear functions. Every question is generated fresh with new numbers, at three levels per topic, with hints, "another way" methods, read-aloud, a Dyslexia mode, and a "what to work on" panel. Progress is saved in your own browser.

Open it: https://eyallowy-stack.github.io/alg2-test2-practice/
